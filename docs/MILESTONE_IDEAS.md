# Milestone Highlight Cards - Idea Catalog

A collection of brainstormed cards for the "Quarm Chronicle / Wrapped" deck:

## 1. 🌟 The Journey & Progression Cards
- **"The Ding Timeline"**: Dates, locations, and time elapsed between key levels (Level 1, 20, 30, 46, 50, 60).
- **"Prestige / NG+ Reset"**: Celebrating turning in level 50 for New Game Plus on Project Quarm.
- **"Frequent Flyer"**: Top 5 most visited zones and hours logged in each.
- **"The Marathon"**: Longest continuous play session recorded.

## 2. ⚔️ Combat & Heroics Cards
- **"Raid Conqueror"**: First-ever recorded kills of legendary raid bosses (Lord Nagafen, Lady Vox, Trakanon, Venril Sathir, Innoruuk, Cazic-Thule).
- **"The Meat Grinder"**: Total damage dealt across career, biggest single crit/blast, and primary combat style.
- **"Lifesaver"**: Total hit points healed and most clutch heals landed (for Cleric/Druid/Shaman).
- **"Floor Inspector"**: Total career deaths, leading cause of death (mob name, poison, drowning, falling damage).
- **"The Clumsy Adventurer"**: Total falling damage taken and number of times slain by gravity.

## 3. 💬 Social & Community Cards
- **"Inner Circle"**: Top 5 friends you exchanged the most tells with (sent vs. received).
- **"The Chatty Cathy"**: Total words typed in guild chat or ooc.
- **"Tunnel Rat"**: Time spent in East Commonlands tunnel and trades conducted.
- **"The Generous Soul"**: Items and platinum given away or traded to other players.

## 4. 🏆 Class-Specific Flavor Cards
- **Bard**: Total songs twisted, Selo's miles run, instruments equipped.
- **Enchanter**: Total mana pumped (Clarity), mez resists survived, charm breaks.
- **Necromancer / Magician**: Pet kills, pets summoned, bone chips consumed.
- **Monk / Feign Death**: FD successes vs. failures, pulling stats.
- **Cleric**: Resurrection count and total experience restored to the guild.
